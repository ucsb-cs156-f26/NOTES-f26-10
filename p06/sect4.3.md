# Section 4.3: Individual vs. Large Team

Summary by Ataman Y.

# Question 1

My experience in school was similar to the one's on the paper. In most of our CS classes group work is prohibited. Our assignments are designed in a way so the student has to work out the problem, implement a solution by code, debug all by themself, and check. Whenever, I am stuck on a problem I prefer to ask my TA's instead of my peers because of source quality and plagiarism possibility. However, inside a professional work environment the paper suggests, it is not possible to finish your work without ever talking to others no matter how qualified you are.

# Question 2

Developers found the amount help you are going to need and teamwork you are going to be involved in surprising. A significant part of their daily lives is to finding correct people to ask their specific questions. People with lack off teamwork experiences felt unprepared, and some realized the importance of personal and time management skills.

# Question 3

Students could join clubs and contest where they can gain teamwork experiences. They could join open source projects where they can work on other people's code instead of building things from scratch by themselves. Moreover, students can form study groups to support each other's academic work. This way they could be better prepared to join workforce as a software developers.
