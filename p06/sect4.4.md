# Section 4.4: Learning vs. User Needs

Summary by Cris M.

# Question 1

I would say that my experiences in school so far have aligned quite well with what the paper reports about academia. Most of the assignments that I have done in my courses have had the intention of helping me learn concepts. While problem solving is very prominent in my assignments, it is not solving the problems of customers. Instead, assignments usually ask to show my understanding of a concept by solving a problem requiring the use of that concept. 

# Question 2

The things that developers found different about industry in terms of the "why" of software development is that the biggest emphasis is on the customer. Developers in industry noted that a lot of their work revolves around customers. They spend a lot of time interviewing them and trying to meet their needs.

# Question 3

 My own idea for what students could do in school to better prepare them for this aspect of software development in industry is to try work on actual projects as much as possible. Students should try and take project based courses, join clubs that do projects, or just work on their own project. I think that this would be a good idea because most courses have you do assignments that are very much in a box. Their main objective is to help you learn concepts. However, with projects, you learn many things along the way with the objective of creating a program that users would want to use. The process forces you to consider user needs which would help a lot with this aspect of software development in industry.
